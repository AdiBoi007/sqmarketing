## Processing notes

- Scene and region artwork uses WebP quality 89 without resizing.
- Avatar crops use lossless WebP and retain their original cream backgrounds, ready for circular CSS masks.
- Inventory object crops use lossless WebP. Cream exterior pixels connected to the crop edges were made transparent for coffee, camera, racket, notebook, mystery box, croissant, and sneakers. Interior colours and original outlines are preserved.
- `character-walker.webp` uses a manually traced polygon alpha mask around the existing main player from `map.webp`. Only the silhouette edge was antialiased; character pixels were preserved.
- `flag.webp` uses a manually traced polygon alpha mask around the original flag and pole from the identity sheet.
- The small inventory objects and avatars should remain small in the composition: their source resolution is intentionally preserved.
- Campus artwork is a stylized existing world illustration. Region crops provide visual mood and continuity, rather than exact geographic replicas.
- `event-society-fair.webp` is an unscaled WebP quality-90 crop of the original society stalls, students and pond. It excludes phone hardware and explanatory labels, and is displayed at or below its native width.
- Source world illustrations include embedded labels and quest markers; these are part of the existing artwork. In broad maps, the source label “Touch Grass” can be covered by a restrained editorial quest annotation to honour the marketing copy brief.

## Official brand exports

The user confirmed `Unknown-6.png` as the official logo sheet. The website uses its original artwork without a redraw, generation, recolouring, or upscaling.

- `brand-wordmark.webp`: primary dark-outline wordmark, including flag, greenery, enclosed cream/white letter pixels, and illustrated blue underline. The exterior parchment and disconnected floating clouds were masked out.
- `brand-sticker.webp`: original bottom-left sticker mark, including its complete white sticker border and all interior illustration pixels. Only the surrounding dark canvas was masked out.
- `brand-app-icon.webp`: original bottom-right app icon, tightly cropped to `(1111, 739, 1388, 1020)` and masked along its original rounded perimeter. Interior pixels were preserved.
- All three exports are lossless WebP. Fully transparent pixels have zero RGB values to avoid transparent-image preview artifacts. Every pixel with nonzero alpha was compared against the corresponding original source crop: zero RGB differences.
- The alpha exports were visually inspected composited over `#f3f0e7` parchment and `#141e28` dark backgrounds.

- `world-journey.webp` is another continuous world perspective from `Unknown-12.png`. Some original poster notes overlap the scenery; these are existing source content.
- `world-riverside.webp` and `world-courts.webp` provide larger environmental quest details from the discovery map.
- `cafe-gathering.webp` includes the full café group illustration beneath the completion headline, excluding that headline and the phone frame. `cafe-storefront.webp` shows the original outside café scene.
- `people-on-campus.webp` and `people-lookout.webp` provide new social scenes. `people-group-cutout.webp` follows the original combined student silhouettes and their original bench/flower occlusions with a manually traced alpha mask.
- Eight larger `collectible-*` assets retain their source illustrations with transparent exteriors. Every nonzero-alpha pixel was checked against its original source crop: zero RGB differences.
- Six `faction-*` assets are small original banner crops excluding their explanatory labels. They retain their original rectangular flag backgrounds and should remain small.
- All new scene crops use WebP quality 88 without resizing. New transparent icons/group assets use lossless WebP. Fully transparent RGB values are zeroed.
- Source world imagery is available as narrow illustrated strips around phone presentations. Keep the new large world scenes near their native width (496–535px), or use a vertical pan/crop, rather than stretching them across a large desktop panorama. The broader earlier campus map remains the highest-resolution landscape source.
